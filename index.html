You belong here

A place to belong, believe and grow.

Hosanna Community Youth is more than a Friday-night gathering. We are a family united by the love of Christ.

We are a community of people from 13 to 60, coming together from different backgrounds and experiences, with one thing in common — a desire to know Christ, live His love and share that love with others.

You don't need to have everything figured out before you walk through our doors.

Whether you've followed Christ for years, are just beginning your journey, or simply want somewhere to belong — there is a place for you here.

Our story

We don't have everything.

But we believe you don't need to have everything to give something.

Hosanna Community Youth exists because we want to expose more people to the love of Christ and create a space where people can encounter that love through both words and actions.

We are a church community that is still growing and finding our way. We have challenges and limitations, but we believe those things don't stop us from serving where we can.

We've had opportunities to spend time at Happy Homes in Kearsney and volunteer at RA Moodley Homes.

We may not always have much to give, but we believe that a little love, a little time, a helping hand and a willing heart can still make a difference.

Being the hands and feet of God's love starts with being willing to show up.

You don't have to walk alone

Lost? Reach out.

Sometimes life becomes heavy. You may feel lost, confused, lonely, overwhelmed, or simply unsure of where to turn.

You don't need to have all the answers. You don't need to have the right words. Just take the first step.

If you need someone to talk to, need guidance in your faith journey, or simply don't know where to begin, reach out to us.

You are seen. You are valued. You are loved. And you don't have to walk alone.
